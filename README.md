I'm **Pavan Kumar Goud Amikula**. I enjoy taking a complex question, breaking it into testable steps, and turning what I learn into an application. My interests connect machine learning, explainable AI, computer vision, and the backend systems that make models usable.

I'm eager to learn from others, work through difficult problems, and contribute to a team. My project reports show the questions I investigate, the approaches I compare, and the limitations I take seriously.

## How I approach problems

- **Understand the problem:** identify the data, failure cases, and practical cost of getting a prediction wrong.
- **Test the alternatives:** compare baselines and architectures under consistent evaluation conditions.
- **Look beyond accuracy:** inspect per-class recall, confusion matrices, imbalance, and the trade-off between performance and efficiency.
- **Explain the result:** explore SHAP, LIME, and Grad-CAM to investigate what influences predictions.
- **Build something usable:** connect experiments to Flask or FastAPI interfaces, then document setup, evidence, and limitations.
